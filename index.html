FAQ

Quick answers to common questions.
Do I need to provide any cleaning supplies?▼
No — we bring everything. Our team arrives fully equipped with professional-grade, eco-friendly cleaning products and all the tools needed.
Do I need to be home during the cleaning?▼
Not necessarily. Many clients provide access instructions and we complete the job while they're out. We'll notify you when we're done.
Are your cleaning products safe for children and pets?▼
Yes. We use eco-friendly, non-toxic products that are safe for kids and pets. If you have specific preferences, just let us know when booking.
What's your cancellation policy?▼
Please give us at least 24 hours notice for cancellations or rescheduling. Late cancellations may incur a fee. We're always happy to reschedule at no cost with enough notice.
What if I'm not satisfied with the clean?▼
Contact us within 24 hours and we'll return to re-clean any areas you're not happy with — completely free, no questions asked.
Which areas do you serve?▼
We primarily serve Urdaneta and nearby areas in Pangasinan. Contact us to confirm if we cover your location.
